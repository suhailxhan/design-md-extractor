# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised below.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic noise
- Anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, Suhail Khan, may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors. Report concerns through GitHub's "Report content" feature, or privately via a security advisory if sensitive. All reports are handled confidentially.
